Core Formulas
Step 1 — Initialization

Set residual to signal

Before any clusters are selected, the entire signal is "unexplained."

r0 = b
S = ∅ (no active clusters)
The residual r holds everything we haven't yet approximated.
Step 2 — Contribution Score

Project residual onto each cluster

For each cluster ℓ, measure how much of r lives in D(ℓ)'s column space:

score(ℓ) = ‖ D(ℓ)ᵀ · r ‖2
D(ℓ)ᵀr gives the projection coefficients. Their ℓ₂ norm measures total alignment with this cluster.
Step 3 — Greedy Selection

Pick the best cluster

Select whichever cluster maximizes the score — it explains the most residual energy:

ℓ* = argmaxℓ∉S ‖ D(ℓ)ᵀ · r ‖2
S ← S ∪ { ℓ* }
This is analogous to OMP (Orthogonal Matching Pursuit) but at the group/cluster level.
Step 4 — Least Squares

Jointly solve over active clusters

Re-fit coefficients for ALL active clusters against the original signal:

xS = argminx ‖ b − DS · x ‖22
where DS = [D(ℓ₁) | D(ℓ₂) | …]
Joint re-solve avoids error accumulation. The solution is: x = (DₛᵀDₛ)⁻¹ Dₛᵀb
Step 5 — Update Residual

Compute new residual

Subtract the new approximation from the signal:

r ← b − DS · xS
Repeat until ‖r‖₂ < ε or |S| = budget
The new r is orthogonal to the span of Dₛ. Energy always decreases or stays the same.
